Preparation

Read Chapters 1–4 of Zipkin (2000) Foundations of Inventory Management and skim Porteus (2002) for notation alignment. Bring recent demand data from your research context for workshop customization.

Learning Portfolio

Maintain a reflective journal that records key insights, derivations, and open questions from each topic. Use the portfolio to propose at least one research extension per lesson.
