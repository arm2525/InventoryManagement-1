Theory Essentials

Duration: 45 minutes References: Wagner & Whitin (1958); Silver, Pyke & Peterson (1998)

The deterministic dynamic lot-sizing problem minimizes total ordering and holding cost over a finite horizon with non-stationary demand. Wagner and Whitin (1958) prove that an optimal solution orders only at periods with zero inventory carry-over and derive an \(O(T^2)\) dynamic program. The recursion evaluates candidate order-up-to points via cost-to-go functions.

In practice, heuristics such as Silver-Meal, Least Unit Cost, and Part-Period Balancing offer near-optimal solutions with lower computational burden. Silver, Pyke, and Peterson (1998) survey performance bounds and hybrid heuristics suitable for rolling-horizon planning.
