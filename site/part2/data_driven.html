Theory Essentials

Duration: 60 minutes References: Bertsimas & Thiele (2006); Levi, Roundy & Shmoys (2007); Oroojlooyjadid et al. (2020)

Robust optimization protects against demand ambiguity by optimizing over uncertainty sets. Bertsimas and Thiele (2006) derive adjustable policies that remain tractable for inventory control. Levi, Roundy, and Shmoys (2007) introduce approximation algorithms for stochastic inventory routing and lost-sales models, highlighting performance guarantees.

Learning-based approaches, such as deep reinforcement learning for newsvendor variants (Oroojlooyjadid et al., 2020), demonstrate how data can continuously refine policies. Combining statistical estimation with prescriptive analytics is central to modern inventory research.
