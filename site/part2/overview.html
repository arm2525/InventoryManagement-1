Schedule at a Glance (4.5 hours)

  • 0:00 – 1:15 — Stochastic dynamic programming and base-stock policies.
  • 1:15 – 2:30 — Multi-echelon coordination and structural results.
  • 2:30 – 3:30 — Service-level driven optimization and contracts.
  • 3:30 – 4:30 — Data-driven, robust, and learning-augmented inventory design.

Preparation

Review Part 1 workshop outputs. Pre-read Scarf (1960) on base-stock optimality and Clark & Scarf (1960) on multi-echelon systems. Prepare to discuss recent research papers from Operations Research and Manufacturing & Service Operations Management.

Integration Challenge

Throughout Part 2, maintain a live policy dashboard (spreadsheet or notebook) that compares the performance of each advanced model under a shared demand scenario. Use it to document the managerial trade-offs and computational requirements of each approach.
