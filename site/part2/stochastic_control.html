Theory Essentials

Duration: 75 minutes References: Scarf (1960); Veinott (1966); Powell (2011)

Scarf (1960) proved that in the single-location periodic-review model with convex costs and i.i.d. demand, an optimal policy is of the (s,S) form. The result hinges on K-convexity of the cost-to-go function, enabling threshold-based replenishment. Veinott (1966) extended structural properties to monotone optimal policies in dynamic programs with multidimensional state.

When state spaces explode, approximate dynamic programming (ADP) provides tractable approximations. Powell (2011) outlines value function approximation and policy search strategies that preserve structural insights while scaling to high-dimensional problems.
