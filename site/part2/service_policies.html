Theory Essentials

Duration: 60 minutes References: Cachon & Lariviere (2005); Banerjee, Burton & Banerjee (2012); Boyaci & Gallego (2004)

Service-level driven policies translate customer requirements into optimization constraints. Cachon and Lariviere (2005) analyze how revenue-sharing and buy-back contracts coordinate service incentives. Boyaci and Gallego (2004) study pricing and lead-time decisions under service targets.

Banerjee, Burton, and Banerjee (2012) extend newsvendor contracts to service-level agreements with penalties, showing how collaborative planning improves both fill rates and profitability.
