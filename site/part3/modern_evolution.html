Milestone Themes

Integrated Supply Chains

Just-in-time, lean, and vendor-managed inventory systems emphasizing coordination (Schonberger, 1982; Cachon & Fisher, 2000).

Stochastic Service Systems

Continuous-review stochastic models with service constraints and lost-sales considerations (Zipkin, 2000; Huh et al., 2009).

Behavioral and Empirical Insights

Empirical testing of inventory decisions and learning from practice (Cachon et al., 2007; Oliva & Watson, 2009).

Data and Technology

Incorporating RFID, real-time data, and analytics into replenishment policies (Atali, Lee & Carr, 2011; Babich & Hilary, 2020).
