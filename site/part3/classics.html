Timeline Highlights

1913 — Harris' EOQ

Introduces the cost trade-off underlying optimal lot sizing for deterministic demand.

1951 — Arrow, Harris & Marschak

Formalizes stochastic single-period inventory decisions via expected utility maximization.

1958 — Wagner & Whitin

Establishes dynamic programming approach to non-stationary lot sizing.

1960 — Scarf and Clark-Scarf

Provide structural optimality results for (s,S) policies and multi-echelon systems.
