Emerging Themes

AI-Augmented Decision Making

Hybrid human-AI systems for inventory control, focusing on interpretability and accountability (Bertsimas & Kallus, 2020).

Sustainable and Circular Supply Chains

Inventory policies that incorporate carbon costs, remanufacturing, and closed-loop flows (Guide & Van Wassenhove, 2009).

Resilience and Disruption Management

Designing adaptive policies under systemic risk, pandemics, and climate uncertainty (Ivanov & Dolgui, 2020).

Behavioral Operations 2.0

Understanding how analytics tools influence managerial biases and decision quality (Gans, 2020).
